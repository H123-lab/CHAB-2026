# CHAB-2026 Data Sharing and Ethical Governance Statement

This document details the ethical parameters, institutional reviews, data permissions, and copyright safety protocols applied to the Cardiology Humanoid AI Benchmark (CHAB-2026).

## 1. Ethics and IRB Determination Statement
Because this study utilized exclusively synthetic, guideline-derived vignettes and text-transformed, de-identified clinical scenarios adapted from published, peer-reviewed public literature, it did not involve active human subjects, animals, or direct patient medical records. No protected health information (PHI) or institutional identifiers were processed. Consequently, under standard academic medical center governance and international regulations, this study was formally determined to be exempt from Institutional Review Board (IRB) oversight.

## 2. Source Material Permissions & Copyright Compliance
- **Guideline-Derived Data (n=56):** Clinical case vectors were synthesized from publicly available consensus guidelines published by the ESC, ACC, and AHA. No copyrighted tables, direct text blocks, or society graphics were reproduced, fully satisfying fair-use metrics and copyright laws.
- **Published Case-Report Data (n=24):** Original published texts were transformed via a rigid, non-automated paraphrasing protocol. Proprietary metadata, specific author names, and concrete calendar dates were completely expunged while preserving core numerical pathophysiological variables exactly, ensuring an open-science audit trail that is entirely free of intellectual property or privacy restrictions.
