# CHAB-2026 Author Contributions and Acknowledgements

This document formalizes project accountability roles according to the CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy) guidelines and recognizes supporting institutional infrastructure.

## 1. Author Contribution Matrix (CRediT Framework)
- **Conceptualization & Study Design:** Lead Curation Panels and Principal Cardiology Investigators.
- **Data Curation & Case Selection Tracking:** Independent expert review panels.
- **Software Architecture & API Execution Pipeline:** Data coordination engineers.
- **Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) Execution:** Principal Medical Statistician.
- **Writing – Original Draft & Editing:** Combined Author Cohort.
- **Project Administration & Governance Oversight:** Academic Cardiology Research Group.
